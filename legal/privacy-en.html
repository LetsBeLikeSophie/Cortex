This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy of Cortex. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. What we collect and how

CategoryData
Kakao sign-inNickname, profile picture URL, Kakao user identifier (to map to your account). Kakao sign-in does not give us your email.
Google sign-inName, email address, profile picture URL, Google account identifier. Your email is used only to identify your account, answer your inquiries and send essential account or payment notices — never for advertising or marketing.
Guest useNo personal information beyond a temporary sign-in token kept on your device.
Content you saveText, links (URLs), screenshots and photos, "my memo" notes you add when saving or later (including ones you dictate), and tags you add yourself — only what you actively choose to save.
SearchWhen you use AI search (✦), the sentence you type or say. It is used only to interpret your search and is not stored. Plain word search is not sent anywhere.
Voice input (microphone)Microphone and speech-recognition permission is requested only when you tap a mic button. Your device's speech-recognition service (Google, on Android) turns speech into text; we never receive or store the audio, only the resulting text.
CameraCamera permission is requested only when you tap "take a photo", and only photos you choose to save are stored.
NotificationsNotification permission is asked once, a week after you first open the app. Reminders (unsorted memos on the 1st of each month, a weekly look-back) are scheduled on your device only; nothing is sent to the server for them. Each can be turned off in My account.
Choosing photosPhotos come in only through your device's own photo picker or another app's "share" button. We do not ask for access to your whole photo library; only the photos you pick are uploaded and saved. Content shared from other appsPhotos, links and text sent to Cortex with another app's "Share" — only what you choose to save is stored. If it came from the photo-cleanup app Mehh, Cortex tells Mehh on your device how many photos were saved, only when you tap [Back to Mehh] on the result screen.
AI consent recordWhether you agreed to AI processing (transfer abroad), when you answered, and which version of the notice you answered.
Paid plan (Pro)Google Play purchase details: product, purchase token, order number, payment state and expiry, and the other details Google returns when we verify a purchase. Card numbers and other payment-method details are handled by Google; we never receive them.
AI result reportsWhen you report something the AI made, we keep that memo's AI-made title, memo and tags and the time of the report — used only to improve AI results and check inappropriate ones.
Memo lockYour memo-lock PIN is stored encrypted on your device only and never sent to us. Fingerprint or face checks are handled by your device; we never receive biometric data. Our server only stores whether each memo is locked, and locked memos are left out of AI search, "compose" and "re-organize with AI".
AI usage recordsWhich AI feature was used, when, and how much was processed — used to apply monthly limits and manage costs. They do not include your content.
Access logsIP address, request time and path, and device/browser information (User-Agent) recorded automatically when you reach our server — used only for security and troubleshooting.
Generated during usePseudonymized usage statistics (a one-way hash that cannot identify your account, event type, category, time). The actual content you save (titles, text, tags) is never part of these statistics.

Screenshots and photos may contain other people's information (conversations, faces, contact details). Please save only what you have the right to save (Terms, Article 7).

2. Why we use it

  • Identifying you, keeping you signed in, and preventing abuse
  • Storing your content, classifying it automatically (titles, categories, tags), and letting you search it and compose memos from it
  • Verifying Pro purchases, managing the paid period, and handling refunds and disputes
  • Applying monthly limits, preventing abuse, and keeping the service secure and running
  • Improving the service through pseudonymized statistics only
  • Answering your inquiries and sending service notices

3. How long we keep it

  1. When you delete your account, your account data (nickname or name, email, profile picture URL, Kakao or Google identifier) and all saved content (including image files) are deleted without delay.
  2. Pseudonymized statistics cannot identify you and may be kept after deletion for statistical purposes only, as permitted by Article 28-2 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.
  3. Your AI consent record, AI result reports and AI usage records are deleted with your account. AI cost records (time and amount) are kept only after your account identifier is removed, so they can no longer be linked to you.
  4. Payment records are kept for 5 years as required by Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, including after account deletion, and then deleted.
  5. Access logs are deleted automatically after 14 days.
  6. Where another law requires us to keep certain records, we keep them for the period the law specifies and then delete them.

4. How we delete it

Electronic records are permanently deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and image files are removed from storage (Supabase Storage) immediately.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law or by a lawful request from investigative authorities. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Transfers to Oracle (hosting) and Google (purchase verification) are necessary to run the service and process payments; if you object to them, you cannot use the service or buy Pro.

7. Users outside Korea

  1. Cortex is operated from the Republic of Korea. Wherever you use it from, your data is processed in the places listed in Section 6 (Korea, Japan, United States). Transfer to the United States for AI processing happens only with the consent you give in the app.
  2. Cortex is currently not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Russia, or Belarus.

8. Your rights

  1. You can view and delete your account and all saved content at any time from the "My account" screen in the app.
  2. For any other request (access, correction, restriction of processing), contact us at the address below and we will act without delay.
  3. Cortex is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information.

9. Security

  • Every request is authenticated with an access token that the server verifies.
  • The database applies per-account (row-level) access control, so you can only read and change your own data.
  • Images are kept in private storage and shown only through short-lived links.
  • Statistics are stored only as a one-way hash that cannot be reversed.
  • All traffic between the app, our server and our providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Secret keys (AI provider, purchase verification) are kept on the server only, never in the app.

10. Local storage

The web version keeps your sign-in token in your browser's localStorage, and the app keeps display settings (theme, home tabs, search mode) on your device. We use no advertising cookies or advertising identifiers.
