  1. Users can delete their account and data at any time on the "My account" screen; it takes effect immediately. Guests can delete their guest data on the same screen. If the app cannot be used, deletion can be requested by email as described on the account deletion page.

If a member who signed in with Kakao disconnects Cortex in Kakao's own settings, the Operator is notified and keeps the account, unable to sign in, for 30 days, then deletes the account and its content as in the paragraph above. Signing in again with the same Kakao account within that time restores it.

After cancelling, no further charges are made from the next renewal date, and Pro stays available until the end of the period already paid for.

If the Operator ends the Service, it will be announced 60 days in advance, and users can export their saved records during that time. The Operator refunds whatever remains of the purchase price after subtracting the number of months used times the monthly subscription price at the time of the announcement.
