Delete your Cortex account

한국어   Privacy Policy

Cortex (developer: Sophie Apps) lets you delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cortex and tap the profile icon at the top right of the home screen (My account).
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom (for a guest: Delete guest data).
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens right away.

If you have a Pro subscription, cancel it in the Google Play Store first (Payments & subscriptions) — deleting the account does not cancel it.

Without the app

Email cortex@itssophie.dev from any address with the subject "Delete my Cortex account" and how you signed in (Kakao, Google or guest). We confirm it is your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name or nickname, email, profile picture URL, Kakao/Google identifier)
  • Everything you saved: text, links, photos/screenshots, notes, tags, combined memos
  • Your AI consent record, plan and monthly AI usage records

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records for Pro purchases: 5 years, as required by Korean e-commerce law
  • AI cost records with your account identifier removed (no longer linked to you)
  • Pseudonymized usage statistics that cannot identify you
  • Server access logs (IP address, time): deleted automatically after 14 days

Deleting only some data

You can delete any single item in the app (it goes to the trash; empty it or delete items permanently from the trash), without deleting your account.
